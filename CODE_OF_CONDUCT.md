# Code of Conduct

Russet adopts the **[Contributor Covenant, version 3.0](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/3/0/code_of_conduct/)**
as its code of conduct. It applies in every project space (the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions,
reviews, chat) and when someone officially represents the project elsewhere. The Covenant's text is incorporated
by reference; this page adds what the Covenant leaves to each project.

## In short

We pledge to make our community welcoming, safe and equitable for all. We respect the purpose of the project,
engage kindly and honestly, respect different viewpoints, take responsibility for our contributions, give and
accept constructive feedback gracefully and repair harm when it occurs. Harassment, character attacks,
stereotyping or discrimination, sexualization, violating confidentiality (including sharing someone's device
identifiers, accounts or other private data), endangerment, impersonation, uncredited copying and spam are not
acceptable. The Covenant's own wording is authoritative.

## Reporting

Report a possible violation privately to the maintainer, [@evilcomputer12](https://github.com/evilcomputer12)
(through GitHub, asking for a private channel), not in a public issue. Reports are handled confidentially; the
maintainer acts as the Community Moderator until the project has more maintainers, and recuses themselves from any
report that concerns them.

## Enforcement

The maintainer follows the Covenant's enforcement ladder: a private written warning; a temporary limit on
activities with a cooldown period; a temporary suspension with conditions for return; and, for a pattern of
violations or a single severe one, a permanent ban. Steps may be skipped depending on severity, and each step names
the repair expected (for example an apology or an acknowledgement of responsibility).
